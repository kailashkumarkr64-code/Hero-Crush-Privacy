# Privacy Policy

## Hero Crush: Battle

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

### Information Stored on Your Device
The game stores your player name, selected country,
language, game progress, rewards and preferences
locally on your device.

### Advertising
The app uses Google AdMob to provide banner,
interstitial and rewarded advertisements.
The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share
IP addresses, approximate location, device
identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic
information for advertising, analytics and
fraud prevention.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

### User Choices
Players can disable banner ads in game settings.
Rewarded advertisements are optional.
Disabling banners does not disable all advertising.
Users can manage advertising preferences through
their Android device settings.

### Data Retention and Deletion
Local game data remains on the device until
the user clears the app storage or uninstalls
the application. Google handles advertising
data according to its own privacy policy.

### Data Security
The app disables Android application backup
and cleartext network traffic.

### Policy Updates
This policy may be updated when application
features or data practices change.
